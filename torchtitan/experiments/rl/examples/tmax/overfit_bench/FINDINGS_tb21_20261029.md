# Teacher overfit runs on seven TB-2.1 tasks: why the originals do not fall yet

2026-10-29. Seven one-task overfit jobs with `teacher_rewriter.py`. Each is a
task the 1026b base never passed (0 of about 60 tries across all its evals).
This note records where each job stands, what is holding the originals back, and
what to try next.

## Setup

- `recipe.env` as written, except:
  - readout runs k=5 (avg@5) instead of 16;
  - training extended to 600 steps.
- LR is a constant 3e-6, group size 12, 8 groups in flight, `SWE_DROP_ZERO_STD=1`.
- Training episodes allow 80K context, 120 turns and 2 h. Readout allows 160K
  context, 300 turns and 5400 s.
- Teacher: one gpt-6.1 thread at high effort, `--near 1`. From step ~190 it also
  runs with `--every 8` (a round after every 8 finished groups, whatever they
  scored; new in dc5b1d516).
- Placement is 2 hosts per job:
  - trainer FSDP-8 on one host;
  - 7 single-GPU generators and the eval generator packed on the other, with gen
    memory at 0.85.

  One host cannot hold this. Each generator gets 1.41M KV tokens against roughly
  4M demanded, so KV sits at 100%, prefix hit rate drops to 0 and requests get
  preempted. On two hosts KV stays near 66%, nothing is preempted and the prefix
  hit rate is 99%. What is left is decode-bound: about 12 ms per token, roughly
  960 tokens per turn.

## What is holding the originals back

1. **Training and readout may not run the same environment (filter-js; check
   the others).**
   - r6 is a byte-for-byte copy of the original: `diff -r` against r0 differs
     only in `status.json`. The tests hash the same, and the sandbox resources
     match (1 CPU, 8 GB).
   - r6 passed 5-10 of 12 in many training groups. Over the same policy steps,
     the original's readout stayed at 0/5.
   - The one difference is the image:
     - readout uses the prebuilt `alexgshaw/filter-js-from-html:20251031`;
     - every teacher version, r0 included, is built fresh from its Dockerfile
       (`image: ""`), and its unpinned `apt-get install chromium` pulls today's
       browser.
   - The XSS test drives chromium, so which payloads fire depends on the browser
     version. Every readout failure is `test_filter_blocks_xss`, and its logs are
     full of chromedriver `Connection refused` retries.
   - Not yet verified: run one passing training `filter.py` against the tests in
     both environments.
2. **Versions do not transfer back to the original, and the original is rarely
   drawn.**
   - Versions reach 40-96% in training, but the original stays near 0 there.
   - Once a curriculum settles, the original's draw weight drops to 0.05-0.15,
     or the original is retired.
3. **The teacher stalls on mid-scoring curricula.**
   - `--near 1` only reacts to groups that end all-fail or all-pass.
   - A pool of versions scoring 40-80% therefore never calls the teacher again,
     and nothing pushes the difficulty back toward the original.
   - `--every N` addresses this. Its effect is still being measured.
4. **Context runs out.**
   - Terminus keeps every past turn's `<think>` in the history, so the final
     context is 1.3-1.9x the total generated tokens.
   - Tool output is not the problem. The 10 KB output fold works, but
     observations are small (p50 360 chars).
   - Readouts ending at the context limit: dna-insert 15, llm-inference 14,
     raman 12.
   - filter-js r6's late collapse was 0/12 groups in which every attempt hit the
     80K limit.
5. **Training caps are tighter than readout caps.**
   - large-scale needs about 192 turns at readout (p50), and llm-inference
     about 106.
   - Training stops at 120 turns and 80K tokens, so training rarely produces a
     complete trajectory for these tasks.
6. **Skill gap.** torch-tensor-parallelism submits every time and is wrong every
   time. Easier versions do not bridge that gap.
7. **Sparse signal.**
   - Groups that end all-pass or all-fail are dropped, so a step trains on about
     one group.
   - A readout takes about 25 minutes, so readouts land only every 10-15 steps.

## What to try next, in order

1. **Check the environment mismatch first.** Run a passing r6 solution in both
   the original image and the Dockerfile build. If the results differ, either
   build teacher versions on the original image or pin the Dockerfile. Then
   audit the other six tasks the same way.
2. **Keep the original in the mix.** Give it a draw-weight floor and never
   retire it. Ask the teacher for near-original versions that each differ from
   the original in one thing.
3. **Tell the teacher how attempts end.** Pass finish-reason counts (context,
   turns, wrong submit) in its news, so its rewrites target the actual failure
   mode.
4. **Context.** Either drop past turns' thinking from the terminus history,
   which cuts context 2-3x but shifts the train/readout distribution, or raise
   the training context toward 160K.
5. **Long-horizon tasks.** Raise the training turn and time caps for
   large-scale and llm-inference.
6. **Skill-gap tasks** (torch-tensor-parallelism). Warm-start from successful
   traces or the reference solution, or use a ladder that teaches the missing
   piece.
7. **Denser signal.** Use larger groups or more groups per step, at the cost of
   compute.

Items 2-7 change the training setup or the harness. They are proposals, not
applied.
