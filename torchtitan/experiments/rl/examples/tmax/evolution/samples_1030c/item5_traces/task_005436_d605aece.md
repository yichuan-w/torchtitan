## Proposed change and new decision

Preserve the load-balancer goal and all existing requirements, but require its self-signed certificate to pass ordinary hostname verification for `localhost` when used as the trust anchor. The changed acceptance behavior is observable from the instruction and pinned client. A CN-only certificate remains plausible and Nginx accepts it, while a verification-enabled request fails; the student must inspect the certificate/client result and decide how to encode the DNS identity. The environment contains the compatibility problem, not a patch or note naming the fix.

This is one modest real-operations step beyond the attempts: they already generate and inspect certificates and repeatedly test HTTPS, but universally use `-k`. The new work is to perform a verified TLS test and regenerate the certificate appropriately, while the backend and Nginx workflow remains unchanged.
